## Premise in one line

All eight Ivies flip from private to public overnight. What actually changes, and what does not?

## Assumptions (disclose)

- 'Public' means state-controlled and state-funded in the IPEDS control sense, not tuition-free. *Disclosure:* On-screen: 'Public ≠ free'

## What changes (hypothetical, must be labeled)

- Every Ivy League university becomes public.
- Each school would likely answer to its state, so residents might get an in-state rate. (leans on v06.f.public_tuition)
- Becoming public would not automatically make them free. (leans on v06.f.public_tuition, v06.a.status)

## Do not say

- Plan rule: do not equate public status with free tuition.
- No invented acceptance rates or tuition figures.
