## Fun take (realistic and funny)

**Vibe:** Culture clash played straight: old New England meets Southern California.

**Destination:** Los Angeles (picked: it sets up a contrast with Boston and puts Harvard next to UCLA).

**Beats:**

1. Harvard was founded in 1636. The brick buildings arrive in Los Angeles and look older than the freeways.
2. Boston gets about 49 inches of snow a year. LA's first Harvard winter involves one confusing cloudy afternoon.
3. Harvard Yard shrinks to fit between LA traffic and parking structures; every student asks where to park.
4. Crimson sweatshirts in 80-degree weather. Shorts quickly become the unofficial uniform.
5. UCLA's Royce Hall is a few miles away, a very different kind of old-looking building.

**Punchline:** Harvard's first LA tradition: complaining about the 405.

**End-card question:** Harvard in LA: upgrade or downgrade?
