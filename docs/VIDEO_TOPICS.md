# Future video brainstorm

Creative proposals following [the creative direction](CREATIVE_DIRECTION.md),
October 31, 2026. These are fictional premises, not forecasts, measured trend
rankings, approved productions, or claims that the current renderer supports
them. Verify real historical anchors before scripting an alternate history.

The original twelve-topic queue remains in [CONTEXT.md](../CONTEXT.md). This
shortlist adds different story mechanisms so the channel can move beyond campus
relocation and weather changes. Asset notes describe possible reuse, not existing
scene support.

The user has since selected the expiring-degree idea. Its V13 prototype is now
implemented as a continuous 2026–2051 film; see [the current status](MVP_STATUS.md).
The remaining ideas below are still proposals.

## Suggested next choices

Start with **the expiring degree** for a human story and a concrete ending, or
**tuition paid in inventions** for a colorful sequence of mechanisms and small
payoffs. Both can reuse more existing components while still requiring new scene
authoring. **The sailing university** is the larger visual adventure to develop
next. **MIT on the Moon** is a later capability investment rather than an
immediate MVP promise.

For the older slate, “every university becomes a country” could become a story
about a student crossing three borders to attend one class; “Stanford never
existed” needs a researched alternate chain of inventions rather than a list of
real companies disappearing. Avoid releasing several near-identical location
swaps back to back.

## Caption approach for these topics

Write the answer and ending before adding suspense. Then identify the next
specific question at each transition: What expires? Who must return? What did the
invention buy? Where is the campus sailing? Answer those questions with visible
events. Keep enough small payoffs that the viewer is rewarded throughout, and
finish with a consequence that makes another story interesting.

Do not copy the opening lines into fixed timings without checking reading load.
These are story directions, not complete scripts, factual findings, or retention
predictions. No new render or automated publishing task is created by this list.
