HISTORICAL ANCHORS (REAL WORLD)
Stanford was founded in California in 1885 and opened in 1891. https://www.stanford.edu/about/history/
Hewlett-Packard’s Palo Alto garage founding dates to 1939. https://www.hp.com/hpinfo/abouthp/histnfacts/garage/brief.pdf
Stanford Industrial Park was authorized in 1951; its first occupant arrived in 1953. https://www-leland.stanford.edu/group/OTL/documents/JSstanfordpark.pdf

The film invents an eastern version of this chain. It does not claim that Stanford alone caused all of Silicon Valley, that earthquakes or other unrelated events disappear, or that the invented future is a forecast.
