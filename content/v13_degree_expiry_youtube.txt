What If Your Degree Expired Every 5 Years?

DESCRIPTION
Your college degree expires. Your knowledge doesn’t.

Imagine a 2026 law that makes every degree expire after five years. Follow one graduate to 2051 as CEOs return to class, factories become classrooms, and staying qualified becomes a lifelong race.

Would you keep renewing your degree—or skip college altogether?
